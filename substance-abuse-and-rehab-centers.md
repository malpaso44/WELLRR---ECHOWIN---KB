1. BUSINESS OVERVIEW
Substance abuse and rehabilitation centers provide treatment and recovery support for alcohol dependency, drug addiction, prescription medication misuse, and co-occurring behavioral health challenges.
The center provides compassionate, confidential treatment tailored to individual needs.
Services may include detox referrals, residential treatment, PHP, IOP, outpatient counseling, individual and group therapy, family support, relapse prevention, and aftercare.
The center serves adults seeking treatment and family members, spouses, parents, employers, and loved ones seeking guidance.
Callers may be experiencing health, relationship, employment, financial, legal, or quality-of-life problems related to substance use.
The goal is to provide support, determine appropriate next steps through assessment, and connect callers with treatment resources.

4. SERVICES OFFERED

Substance Abuse Assessment

Evaluates substance-use history and treatment needs.
Used to help determine an appropriate level of care.

Medical Detox Referral

Referral and coordination with licensed detox facilities when medically appropriate.
Intended to support safe stabilization before treatment.

Residential / Inpatient Treatment

Structured live-in treatment with 24-hour care and supervision.
Supports early recovery stabilization.

Partial Hospitalization Program (PHP)

Intensive daytime treatment while living at home or supportive housing.
Provides a high level of support without inpatient residency.

Intensive Outpatient Program (IOP)

Flexible treatment for clients balancing recovery with work, family, or school.

Outpatient Counseling

Appropriate for early-stage concerns, recovery maintenance, recent treatment completion, or lower relapse risk.

Individual Counseling

One-on-one therapy addressing personal challenges and triggers.

Group Therapy

Facilitated peer support promoting accountability and recovery skills.

Family Counseling

Education and support addressing relationships affected by addiction.

Relapse Prevention

Education and planning intended to support continued sobriety.

Aftercare Planning

Ongoing support following formal treatment.

19. TREATMENT INFORMATION

Medical Detoxification

Initial physical clearing of substances from the body.
Withdrawal can be intense and sometimes dangerous.
Medical detox occurs in a monitored setting where doctors and nurses may use prescription medications to manage symptoms, reduce cravings, and support stabilization.
Detox alone does not treat addiction; it prepares the patient for continued treatment.

Levels of Care

Residential / Inpatient → Lives on-site; 24/7 care and supervision; for high-risk cases, severe addiction, or unsafe home environments.
PHP → Lives at home or sober living; approximately 5–8 hours daily, 5+ days weekly; intensive therapy without residential care.
IOP → Lives at home; approximately 2–3 hours daily, 3–4 days weekly; supports recovery while balancing work, school, or family.

Behavioral Therapies

CBT → Identifies connections among thoughts, feelings, actions, triggers, and coping responses.
DBT → Focuses on mindfulness, distress tolerance, and managing difficult emotions.
Motivational Interviewing → Helps patients explore mixed feelings about change and identify personal reasons for recovery.

Medication-Assisted Treatment / MAT

Combines behavioral therapy with FDA-approved medications.
May be used for certain opioid and alcohol addictions.
Medication decisions are made by qualified medical professionals.

Aftercare & Relapse Prevention

May include outpatient therapists, psychiatrists, sober living, alumni networks, peer support, 12-step programs, or SMART Recovery.
Every service begins with an intake assessment covering physical health, mental health history, and substance-use patterns.
The assessment determines the individualized care plan.
