### 9. PERSONAL INJURY QUALIFICATION MATRIX

**PRIORITY A — IMMEDIATE ATTORNEY REVIEW**

Triggers:
- Wrongful death
- Traumatic brain injury
- Paralysis
- Spinal cord injury
- Amputation
- Severe burns
- Permanent disability
- Commercial truck accident
- Multiple victims / mass-injury incident

Action:
- Immediate live transfer to Intake Manager, Senior Case Manager, or Attorney.
- Wrongful death: if unavailable, flag HIGH PRIORITY and schedule same-day attorney consultation.
- Catastrophic injury: if unavailable, schedule attorney consultation within 24 hours.
- Commercial truck accident: priority attorney review; schedule within 24 hours if no live transfer is available.
- Multiple victims: escalate immediately.

**PRIORITY B — HIGH-VALUE CASE**

Requirements:
- Clear injury
- Medical treatment received
- Another party may be at fault

Indicators:
- Surgery
- Hospitalization
- Broken bones
- Extended treatment
- Significant lost wages

Action:
- Priority consultation within 24–48 hours.
- Lead Status: HIGH VALUE

**PRIORITY C — STANDARD QUALIFIED CASE**

Requirements:
- Accident occurred
- Injury reported
- Medical evaluation completed or scheduled

Examples:
- Auto accident
- Slip and fall
- Dog bite
- Pedestrian accident
- Bicycle accident

Action:
- Book standard consultation.
- Lead Status: QUALIFIED

**PRIORITY D — REVIEW NEEDED**

Examples:
- No medical treatment yet
- Minor injuries
- Fault unclear
- Limited documentation

Action:
- Book attorney-review consultation.
- Lead Status: REVIEW NEEDED

**NOT QUALIFIED**

Examples:
- No injury
- No damages
- Criminal defense
- Family law
- Business dispute
- Bankruptcy
- Estate planning

Action:
- Politely explain that the firm may not handle the matter.
- Use referral protocol if available.
- Lead Status: NOT QUALIFIED

### 10. INSURANCE, MEDICAL & CASE-PRIORITY RULES

**Insurance**
- Insurance known: collect company, claim number, and adjuster information; proceed normally.
- Insurance unknown: continue intake. Do not disqualify. Attorney can review coverage options.
- Uninsured at-fault driver: continue intake. A potential claim may still exist. Book consultation.

**Medical Treatment**
- ER visit → HIGH → Book immediately.
- Hospital admission → VERY HIGH → Priority consultation.
- Surgery recommended → VERY HIGH → Priority attorney review.
- Ongoing physical therapy → MEDIUM-HIGH → Qualified consultation.
- No treatment yet → Still book consultation and mark for attorney review.

**High-Value Indicators**
- Ambulance transport
- Hospital admission
- Surgery
- Permanent injury
- Lost wages
- Disability
- Commercial vehicle involved
- Multiple defendants

Action:

### 11. ATTORNEY REPRESENTATION & DEADLINE ALERTS
- Already has attorney → Route to management review. Do not book standard consultation.
- Previously had attorney → Attorney review required. Book consultation.
- Never had attorney → Proceed with standard intake.
- Accident more than 1 year ago, more than 2 years ago, or possibly near filing deadline → Flag URGENT REVIEW and require attorney consultation.
- Never advise caller about legal deadlines.

### 18. PERSONAL INJURY PROCESS
1. Initial Consultation & Hiring → Caller discusses what happened. Many personal injury lawyers use contingency fees, meaning no upfront attorney fee and payment is generally a percentage of a recovery.
2. Investigation & Treatment → Firm gathers medical records, bills, lost-wage evidence, and other information while treatment progresses.
3. Demand Letter → Once treatment is stable, lawyer may present evidence, injuries, and requested settlement amount to insurer.
4. Discovery & Negotiation → If a lawsuit is filed, both sides exchange evidence, may take depositions, and continue negotiations.
5. Settlement or Trial → Case may resolve through settlement or proceed to trial if the parties cannot agree.
